FILM 15″
OFF DUTY

No dialogue. No aspirational sunrise. Just a wellness checklist behaving like a new employer.

00–02

Clock in.

Alarm. A hand grabs the phone. Before he is fully awake, an enormous wellness checklist fills the screen.

Alarm. Room tone.
02–05

The workload.

Rapid cuts: SUNLIGHT. BREATHWORK. JOURNAL. COLD SHOWER. PROTEIN. GREENS. STEPS. MEDITATE. GRATITUDE. He scrolls. It keeps going.

Tiny notification pings stacking up.
05–07

New job, apparently.

He sits on the edge of the bed staring at the list like someone has added him to a project he never agreed to join.

Silence.
07–10

Three seconds.

Kitchen. Scoop Rheal. Stir. Drink. No ceremony. No wellness influencer lighting.

Spoon. Glass. Tap water.
10–12

Resignation.

Back to the phone. Long-press on the wellness checklist folder. DELETE.

One dry phone click.
12–15

End card.

WELLNESS HAS BECOME A FULL-TIME JOB.

YOU CAN QUIT NOW.

RHEAL.

Nothing. Let it hang.
